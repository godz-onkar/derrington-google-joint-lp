FAQ

Questions people ask first

What does it cost, and does insurance cover it?

Treatment is self-pay, and insurance doesn’t cover it. You can use HSA or FSA funds, or a payment plan through Cherry or CareCredit. The price depends on what Dr. Derrington finds when he examines you, so there’s no single number until he’s built your plan.

How long before I feel better?

Results vary more than anyone would like. Some people notice a change in a few weeks and others take a few months. Dr. Derrington tells you what’s reasonable for your condition before you commit to anything.

What if my joint is too far gone?

That’s what the consultation is for. Some joints are past the point where this helps, and Dr. Derrington will tell you plainly if yours is.

I had an injection somewhere else and it didn’t help.

That happens, and it’s worth a conversation. When an injection fails, the cause is usually the wrong spot, a needle placed by feel instead of under imaging, or a dose too weak to do much. None of that is a verdict on your body.

Do I need an MRI or X-ray?

No, you don’t need imaging to start. If you already have an MRI or X-ray, bring it to your consultation and Dr. Derrington will go through it with you.
